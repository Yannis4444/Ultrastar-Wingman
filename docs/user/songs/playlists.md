# Playlists

A playlist is a named, ordered list of songs: the birthday set, the ballads,
everything for the late shift. A playlist belongs to an account, so making one
takes a login.

Open :wingman-list: **Playlists** at the end of the chip row on the **Songs**
tab. Each row carries the first covers of the list as a small mosaic. Beside
them stand the name, how many songs are on it, and how long they run. A row that
is not yours also names its owner. :wingman-globe: beside the name means
everybody in the party can see the list.

The page sorts what you can see into groups. A group with nothing in it is not
shown.

| Group | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Your playlists** | The ones you made |
| **Shared with you** | The ones somebody named you on and you kept |
| **Global** | The ones everybody in the party can see |
| **Hidden** | Shared lists you unfollowed, folded away |

!!! note
    Favorites are not a playlist. They are yours alone, nobody can share them,
    and they live on the song itself. See [Open a song](song.md).

## Make one

1. Tap :wingman-plus: **New playlist** at the top of the playlists page.
2. Type a **Name**, and a **Description** if the list needs one.
3. To let the whole party see it, select **Global: everyone can see it**.
4. Tap **Create**. Wingman opens the new list so you can start filling it.

## Add and order songs

Open the playlist. Under its name stands one line saying how big the list is and
whether it is global. It also names who shared it and what you may do with it.
The buttons below it are the ones you may use.

- :wingman-list-plus: **Add songs** searches the whole library. Tap a song to
  put it on the list, tap it again to take it off.
- :wingman-pencil: **Edit** turns every row into a reorder row. Drag it by the
  grip, or use :wingman-arrow-up: **Move up** and :wingman-arrow-down: **Move
  down**. :wingman-close: **Remove** takes the song off the list. The new order
  saves itself as you make it. Tap :wingman-pencil: **Done** when you have
  finished.

A song also goes on a list from the song itself. :wingman-list-plus: **Add to
playlist** in a song's view lists the playlists you may edit. Tap one to add the
song, tap it again to take it out. :wingman-list-check: marks the lists that
already hold it.

**New playlist** at the foot of that list turns into a name field. Type a name,
tap :wingman-plus: **Create the playlist and add the song**, and the song lands
on a private list of its own.

## Share a playlist

:wingman-gear: **Manage playlist**, at the top right of a playlist, is where
sharing happens. It is there on the lists you made and on a list somebody shared
with you as an admin. A playlist moderator gets it on any list they can already
see.

**Name**, **Description**, and **Global: everyone can see it** are the top of
the page. **Save** sends only what you changed. Seeing a list is not changing
it, so the **Global** box hands nobody any rights.

Under **Members** stands the owner first, then everybody the list was shared
with. Type at least two letters of a username into **Add people** to find an
account. The search matches the beginning of a name. Tap :wingman-user-plus:
**Share with** beside a name to share the list with that person as a reader.

After that, the drop-down on their row decides what they may do, and
:wingman-close: **Remove** takes them off. Their access goes and the songs stay.

| Access | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **read** | See the list and its songs |
| **write** | Add songs, take songs off, change the order |
| **admin** | All of that, plus renaming, sharing, and deleting the list |

Whoever may moderate playlists can change any list they can already see: the
global ones, and the ones shared with them. Trusted and Admin hold that by
default. It never reaches a list nobody shared with them.

## Follow, copy, spin, delete

- :wingman-bookmark-off: **Unfollow** moves a list somebody shared with you into
  **Hidden**. :wingman-bookmark: **Follow** brings it back. The access stays and
  nobody is told.
- :wingman-copy: **Save a copy** at the foot copies any playlist you can see.
  The copy has the same songs in the same order, is yours, and is shared with
  nobody.
- :wingman-dice: **Random song from this playlist** spins the reel over this
  list alone. It appears once the list holds a song. See
  [Let the reel pick](../party/reel.md).
- **Delete playlist**, at the foot of the manage page, asks once and then takes
  the list away from everybody it was shared with. The songs themselves stay in
  the library.
