# Let the reel pick

Nobody can decide what to sing? **Random song** on **Home** opens a reel that
picks for you. It spins like a slot machine and stops on the winner.

A playlist has its own reel: the dice at the top right of a playlist spins that
playlist alone.

## Spin it

1. Choose what it may pick from. The line under the choices tells you how
   many songs are in the running.
2. Press **SPIN**.
3. The reel runs for a few seconds and reveals the song. Tap the winner to open
   its details — from there you go straight into **Sing**.

## What it picks from

| Choice | What can win |
|---|---|
| **All songs** | Every song in the library |
| **Wishlist** | Only wished songs, each with the same chance |
| **Wishlist (weighted)** | Only wished songs, more wishes mean a better chance |

Weighted is the fair one for a full room: a song three people wished for has
three times the chance of a song only one person wished for.

Both wishlist choices stay greyed out while nobody has wished for anything.

## One playlist's reel

Opened from a playlist's dice, the reel picks from that playlist and nothing
else — there are no choices above it, only the number of songs it can land on.
The dice appears once the playlist has a song in it.

!!! note
    A phone set to reduce motion skips the animation and shows the winner right
    away. The pick is the same either way.
