# Sing a song

Starting a song takes two steps: pick the song, then say who sings it. Ultrastar
Deluxe does the rest.

## Find the song

Open the **Songs** tab. It lists everything Wingman found in your song folders,
with the number of songs at the top.

- The search box matches title and artist while you type. A song id also works.
- The chips below it narrow the list: **All**, **♥ Favorites**, **Recent** and
  **Sung**. Only the chip you are standing on spells its name out; the others
  shrink to their icon so the row keeps fitting a phone. The list icon at the
  end of the row opens the playlists view, where a playlist shows its own songs.
- The reload button next to the song count scans the song folders again.
- Tap a song to open it: the cover, a listen-in preview, and the buttons for
  favorite, sing and wish. Below them, **Details** unfolds language, genre,
  year, edition and who made it; **Best scores** lists the best runs ever sung
  on it; **Add to playlist** puts the song on one of the playlists you may edit;
  **Tags** shows the labels the party has put on it, and lets you set the ones
  you are allowed to.

## What has already been sung

**Recent** lists the songs of the party that is going on right now, the one
played last at the top, with the time it started next to it. A song that was
sung twice appears once, at the later time.

The chip only appears once there is something to show. It counts songs that were
*started*, so a song somebody stopped after one verse is in the list even though
Ultrastar Deluxe never scored it. Six hours without a song end the party, and the
chip disappears again with the next reload — if you are standing on the tab when
that happens it stays put and simply says nothing has been sung yet.

The search box works inside the tab, and tapping a row opens the song as usual —
handy when the room wants the same song again.

## What you have sung before

A small blue microphone next to a title means you have sung that song before, in
this party or in any party Wingman ever recorded. The **Sung** chip filters the
list down to those songs.

Both need an account with a player behind it: a song counts as sung by you once
Ultrastar Deluxe scored it under your player. A song nobody scored — stopped
after a verse, or sung with no microphone plugged in — belongs to nobody in
particular, so it does not show up here. It is still in **Recent**.

## The marks next to a title

A row can carry a few small icons after the song's title. They are marks, not
buttons — nothing happens when you tap one, and the song's own view is where the
state is changed.

- A blue **microphone** means you have sung this song before — the section
  above.
- A pink **heart** means it is one of your favorites.
- After those, one icon per **tag** the song wears that was set up to show in the
  list — in the tag's own colour, with its name on hover. A tag nobody chose to
  show stays out of the row and lives in the song's **Tags** view.

## Songs you do not see

Some songs are deliberately kept out of the list.

- A tag can be set to **hide songs by default**. Its songs are out of the song
  list — in every chip, not only *All* — and out of the random reel, until the
  tag filter asks for them. They are still there: a playlist that holds one
  still shows it, because somebody put it there on purpose. The seeded
  **archive** tag works this way.
- A tag can also take *See songs* away from a role entirely. Those songs do not
  exist for that person at all: not in the list, not in the search, not in the
  reel. The seeded **broken** tag works this way, so nobody stumbles into a song
  that does not play.

If a filter ends up empty because of the first kind, the page says so instead of
pretending you have no favorites. See [Tags](tags.md) for the whole picture.

## Choose the players

Press **Sing** in the song details. The player selection opens.

1. The coloured boxes are the microphone slots, in the order Ultrastar Deluxe
   uses them. The first slot is blue, the second red, and so on.
2. Tap a player below to put them on the next free slot. Tap them again to take
   them off. Tap a slot first to fill exactly that slot.
3. With more than twelve players there is a **Filter players** box above the
   list. Type any part of a name and only the matching people stay visible.
   Clearing the box brings everybody back. Filtering only hides rows — nobody
   loses their slot by being filtered away.
4. Missing somebody? Type a name into **New player** and press **Add**. The new
   player goes straight onto a slot.
5. Press **Play**.

Ultrastar Deluxe opens on the TV with that song, and with the chosen names,
colours and avatars already set up. At least one slot needs a player.

!!! note
    Your slot assignment is remembered in your browser. The same group does not
    have to pick again for every song.

## Players, colours and avatars

Players live under **User → Players & accounts**, in two groups: the people with
an account, and the guests who only sing.

- **Add** a player by name, under **Guests**. Names are unique.
- Tapping a player's picture uploads a new one — jpeg, png or webp, up to 5 MB
  and 50 megapixels. Ultrastar Deluxe shows it next to the name while singing.
  Your own picture can also be changed by tapping it on the **User** tab.
- The bin removes a guest. Scores that player already earned keep the name.
  Somebody with an account is removed by deleting the account, which turns them
  into a guest first.

A registered account automatically has a player of the same name, which is why
they stand under **Accounts**. Somebody who sang all evening as a guest keeps
their scores when they register under that exact name later.

The colours are the sixteen Ultrastar Deluxe player colours, and Wingman uses the
same order Ultrastar Deluxe does. There is nothing to configure.

## While a song plays

**Home** shows what is playing; the host screen also shows how far in it is and
who is on which colour. On **Home** the running song takes the place of the
Wingman title at the top of the page — cover, title and artist, with the buttons
below — and hands the title back when the song ends. The time and the singers
are not repeated there: the Ultrastar Deluxe screen in the same room already
shows both. The tile keeps its size either way, so nothing under it jumps.

- **Stop** is the large pink button in the middle, and it asks before it ends
  the song. Only somebody who may stop a song sees it at all. The heart and the
  wish button beside it do the same as in the song's own view.
- Starting another song while one is running offers to replace it — replacing
  stops the running one first. Whoever may not stop a song is told to ask the
  host instead.
- The scores appear on the **Scores** tab shortly after Ultrastar Deluxe closes.
  Wingman reads them from Ultrastar Deluxe and files them under the players it
  put on the slots.

!!! warning
    An unfinished song scores nothing. Ultrastar Deluxe only writes a score when
    a song is sung to the end, so a stopped song leaves no result behind.
