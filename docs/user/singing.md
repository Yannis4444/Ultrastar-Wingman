# Sing a song

Starting a song takes two steps: pick the song, then say who sings it. Ultrastar
Deluxe does the rest.

## Find the song

Open the **Songs** tab. It lists everything Wingman found in your song folders,
with the number of songs at the top.

- The search box matches title and artist while you type. A song id also works.
- The chips below it narrow the list: **All** and **♥ Favorites**. **Playlists**
  next to them opens the playlists view, where a playlist shows its own songs.
- The reload button next to the song count scans the song folders again.
- Tap a song to open it: the cover, a listen-in preview, and the buttons for
  favorite, sing and wish. Below them, **Details** unfolds language, genre,
  year, edition and who made it; **Best scores** lists the best runs ever sung
  on it; **Add to playlist** puts the song on one of your playlists.

## Choose the players

Press **Sing** in the song details. The player selection opens.

1. The coloured boxes are the microphone slots, in the order Ultrastar Deluxe
   uses them. The first slot is blue, the second red, and so on.
2. Tap a player below to put them on the next free slot. Tap them again to take
   them off. Tap a slot first to fill exactly that slot.
3. With more than twelve players there is a **Filter players** box above the
   list. Type any part of a name and only the matching people stay visible.
   Clearing the box brings everybody back. Filtering only hides rows — nobody
   loses their slot by being filtered away.
4. Missing somebody? Type a name into **New player** and press **Add**. The new
   player goes straight onto a slot.
5. Press **Play**.

Ultrastar Deluxe opens on the TV with that song, and with the chosen names,
colours and avatars already set up. At least one slot needs a player.

!!! note
    Your slot assignment is remembered in your browser. The same group does not
    have to pick again for every song.

## Players, colours and avatars

Players live under **User → Players & accounts**, in two groups: the people with
an account, and the guests who only sing.

- **Add** a player by name, under **Guests**. Names are unique.
- Tapping a player's picture uploads a new one — jpeg, png or webp, up to 5 MB
  and 50 megapixels. Ultrastar Deluxe shows it next to the name while singing.
  Your own picture can also be changed by tapping it on the **User** tab.
- The bin removes a guest. Scores that player already earned keep the name.
  Somebody with an account is removed by deleting the account, which turns them
  into a guest first.

A registered account automatically has a player of the same name, which is why
they stand under **Accounts**. Somebody who sang all evening as a guest keeps
their scores when they register under that exact name later.

The colours are the sixteen Ultrastar Deluxe player colours, and Wingman uses the
same order Ultrastar Deluxe does. There is nothing to configure.

## While a song plays

**Home** and the host screen show what is playing and who is on which colour.
On **Home** the running song takes the place of the Wingman title at the top of
the page — cover, title, how far in it is, and the singers on their colours —
and hands the title back when the song ends. The tile keeps its size either way,
so nothing under it jumps.

- **Stop** is the large pink button in the middle, and it asks before it ends
  the song. Only somebody who may stop a song sees it at all. The heart and the
  wish button beside it do the same as in the song's own view.
- Starting another song while one is running offers to replace it — replacing
  stops the running one first. Whoever may not stop a song is told to ask the
  host instead.
- The scores appear on the **Scores** tab shortly after Ultrastar Deluxe closes.
  Wingman reads them from Ultrastar Deluxe and files them under the players it
  put on the slots.

!!! warning
    An unfinished song scores nothing. Ultrastar Deluxe only writes a score when
    a song is sung to the end, so a stopped song leaves no result behind.
